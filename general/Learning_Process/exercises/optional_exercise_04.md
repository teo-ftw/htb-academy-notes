## What is the Learning Pyramid?

The learning pyramid is a pseudoscientific group of effective learning models and representations relating different degrees of retention induced from various types of learning.

## Learning Activities

These are the learning activiteis presented in the learning pyramid sorted by the retention rate:

1. Teach someone else / use immediately. -- 90%
2. Practice what one learned. -- 75%
3. Engage in a group discussion. -- 50%
4. Watch a demonstration. -- 30%
5. Watch audiovisual. -- 20%
6. Read. -- 10%
7. Listen to a lecture. -- 5%

## History of the Learning Pyramid

The earliest representation is believed to originate in a 1954 book called "Audio-Visual Methods in Teaching."
