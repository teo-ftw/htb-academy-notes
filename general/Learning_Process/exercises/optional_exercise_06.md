I have chosen to read the penetration testing report [Pentest Logback RC1.1](https://www.pentestreports.com/reports/7asecurity/pentest-logback-RC1.1) by 7asecurity on [Pentest Reports](https://www.pentestreports.com).

## Overview

### 1. What topics have been covered?

The penetration testing report covers the topics of identified vulnerabilities, hardening recommendations, WP2: Logback Lightweight Threat Model and WP3: Logback Supply Chain Implementation. (All taken from the table of contents of the pentest report.)

### 2. How are they structured?

The topics are structured in a hierarchical format, each topic leading into the next one.

### 3. How are they presented?

The topics are presented in an organized fashion, with headings, highlights and bold text to emphasise the importance of the respective subject being discussed. Furthermore, several tables indicating simple and clear terms are displayed accross the whole report. There are also multiple sections extracted from the terminal showcasing output text.
