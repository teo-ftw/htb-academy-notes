# Intro to Academy

## HTB Academy Structure

1. Path
2. Modules
3. Sections

*Note:* Some paths are linked to certifications, which can be obtained after completeing their path and **exam.**

## Modules

### Types of Modules

1. Offensive (red)
2. Defensive (blue)
3. Purple (both offensive and defensive)
4. General

Difficulties:
1. Fundamental
2. Easy
3. Medium
4. Hard

### Completing a Module

Solve all exercises, mark all sections as "completed" and face the module's **Skills Assessment.**
At the end, press **"Finish."**

## Sections

### Types of Sections

1. Theory
2. Interactive (They have questions and exercises at the end.)

## Exercises

Exercises are practical examples performed on a live target which you can spawn by pressing the **"Spawn the target system"** button.
